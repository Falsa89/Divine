## 1. Giudizio di Asgard
Type: competitive survival gauntlet.

Divine mechanics:
- Player faces 10 user rosters of similar level/power.
- Future cross-server use is possible only after SLC maturity.
- HP, rage and team state do not fully reset between battles.
- Small controlled buffs may be offered between fights.
- Milestone rewards at 33%, 66%, 100% completion.
- The mode measures survival, roster consistency and tactical endurance.

Benchmark inspiration:
- Epic Seven Dagger Sicar: challenge-board style scoring.
- Future Fight World Event: score-focused challenge concepts.
- Raid/Faction Wars: staged restrictions, but must be lighter.

Do not import:
- cross-server mismatch before SLC is ready;
- paid revive/attempts that decide ranking;
- exact premium hero requirements.

## 2. Cammino dell’Ade
Confirmed benchmark: Bleach Online Hueco Mundo Attack.

Type: branching survival route.

Divine mechanics:
- Progressive run with branching paths.
- Encounters include enemy teams, shops, healing shrines and resurrection shrines.
- Player earns a mode-specific currency.
- Player chooses 1 of 3 random buffs valid only during the current run.
- HP, rage and team state persist between battles.
- Fallen heroes are recovered only by run-specific recovery systems.

Import from benchmark:
- survival ladder feeling;
- daily/reset structure;
- coin exchange shop;
- progressive enemy sequence;
- controlled revive/reset mechanics.

Do not import:
- paid revives decisive for ranking;
- excessive premium resets;
- unfair random enemy power mismatch.

## 3. Scala dell’Olimpo
Type: wave/endurance progression.

Divine mechanics:
- Consecutive waves.
- Classic version and elemental variants: Fuoco, Acqua, etc.
- HP and rage persist.
- Intermediate buffs.
- Leaderboards.
- Next day starts slightly below previous max progress, not fully from zero.

Benchmark inspiration:
- Summoners War Trial of Ascension: milestone/boss progression.
- Idle Angels Sky-tower: fastest clear / lowest-power clear / top ranking references.
- AFK Arena towers: long-term progression scaling.

Do not import:
- daily chore overload;
- impossible element roster requirements too early;
- reward dependence that makes the mode mandatory every day.

## 4. Sigilli degli Dei
Type: short tactical special-rule sequences.

Divine mechanics:
- Small sequences of battles.
- Rules can constrain elements, roles, factions, or team composition.
- Periodic rewards.
- Focus on adaptation and roster flexibility, not long tower progression.

Benchmark inspiration:
- Bleach Brave Souls Senkaimon special rules.
- Raid Faction Wars, but less restrictive.
- Omniheroes Celestial Trials.
- Genshin Imaginarium Theater restrictions, lighter.

Do not import:
- exact rare hero gates;
- too many restrictions at once;
- confusing the mode with a simple material dungeon.

## 5. Torre degli Inferi
Confirmed benchmarks:
- Sword Art Online Memory Defrag Floor Clearing Castle.
- Bleach Brave Souls Senkaimon.

Type: seasonal floor tower with roster lock mechanics.

Divine mechanics:
- Seasonal floor climb.
- Boss floors.
- Some bosses can lock used heroes.
- Locked heroes can be recovered by challenging that boss again with a different team.
- Rollout progression: 25 floors → 50 → 75 → 100.

Import from SAO MD:
- floor clearing identity;
- sector/floor pacing;
- limited hero usage;
- rare treasure floor concept.

Import from BBS Senkaimon:
- special floor rules;
- medal exchange;
- clear-time tracking;
- readable floor requirements.

Do not import:
- excessive lock frustration;
- missable critical power rewards;
- floor conditions too narrow.

## 6. Troni dell’Eclissi
Type: seasonal multi-boss modifier/ranking mode.

Divine mechanics:
- Multiple rotating bosses.
- Boss-specific leaderboards.
- Overall seasonal ranking.
- Seasonal modifiers, mainly elemental.
- Rewards from progression and ranking.

Important correction:
- This is NOT Seireitei Soul Palace.

Benchmark inspiration:
- Nikke Solo Raid / Union Raid: boss ranking structure.
- Raid Hydra-style multi-boss, simplified.
- Epic Seven Advent boss: multi-phase boss concepts.

Do not import:
- whale-only ranking-critical rewards;
- too many boss mechanics at once;
- elemental modifiers that invalidate most rosters.

## 7. Prove del Pantheon
Type: objective/completion tactical challenge.

Divine mechanics:
- Battles have specific objectives.
- Examples: win within X turns, no fallen hero, required composition.
- Rewards include clear reward and bonus objective rewards.

Benchmark inspiration:
- Epic Seven Dagger Sicar.
- Genshin challenge objectives.
- Raid progression/challenge missions.
- Bleach Brave Souls Challenge Orders.

Do not import:
- objectives requiring exact premium units;
- too many simultaneous objectives;
- permanent power behind extreme objectives.

## 8. Abisso del Colosso
Type: individual boss/survival ladder.

Divine mechanics:
- Consecutive boss or mini-boss sequence.
- HP/rage/team state does not fully reset.
- Focus on damage, endurance and personal progression.

Benchmark inspiration:
- Raid Doom Tower bosses.
- Mythic Heroes Hades’ Hell.
- Summoners War boss/dungeon progression.
- Future Fight World Boss material progression.

Do not import:
- server-wide boss identity; this is individual/progression.
- ranking-only core rewards;
- overly wide roster pressure too early.

## 9. Crepuscolo dei Titani
Confirmed benchmark: Bleach Void Region.

Type: server-wide titan/boss event.

Divine mechanics:
- All eligible players fight one huge central boss.
- Rewards: participation, damage contribution, kill contribution.
- Respawn timer: 60 seconds.
- Schedule: Monday / Wednesday / Friday, 20:30–21:30.

Benchmark imports:
- server-wide boss pressure;
- contribution-based rewards;
- damage ranking;
- respawn/return pacing.

Do not import:
- final blow as major reward;
- boss dying instantly due to whales;
- timezone-exclusion design.

## 10. Giudizio delle Stirpi
Confirmed benchmark: Bleach Online Evil Spirit.

Type: faction-flavored live boss event.

Divine mechanics:
- Each faction faces a boss derived from the strongest team of one of its own players.
- That team is cloned and empowered.
- After defeating own faction boss, players can participate against other faction bosses.
- Rewards: participation, damage, kill.
- Autoplay supported.
- Schedule: 09:00–10:00 and 14:00–15:00.
- One rewarded participation per day across both windows.

Import from benchmark:
- timed daily event format;
- fast contribution loop;
- live boss/miniboss pressure.

Do not import:
- impossible strongest-player clones;
- faction imbalance snowball;
- multi-boss reward abuse.

## 11. Fronti del Valhalla
Confirmed benchmark: Bleach Online Guild War.

Type: guild lane/front war.

Divine mechanics:
- Battle lines/fronts.
- Progressive advance.
- Instant battles.
- Battle reports viewable.
- Points from kills, streaks and line completion.
- Rewards from guild placement and internal guild ranking.
- Schedule: daily 17:00–18:00.
- Autoplay useful.

Import from benchmark:
- registration;
- lane/path selection;
- auto-resolved clashes;
- win streak scoring;
- bonus for stopping enemy streak;
- personal + guild ranking.

Do not import:
- paid cooldown clear;
- paid morale advantage;
- VIP skip in competitive mode.

## 12. Guerra dei Tre Troni
Confirmed benchmark: Marvel Future Fight Alliance Conquest.

Type: three-guild territory conquest.

Divine mechanics:
- 3 guilds on zone map.
- Spawn points: north, south-east, south-west.
- Only adjacent zones can be conquered.
- Controlled zones generate points over time.
- 3 special zones give important but non-dominant buffs.
- Battle days: Tuesday / Thursday / Saturday, 22:00–23:00.
- Prep days: Monday / Wednesday / Friday.
- Prep tools: map preview, special zone preview, defensive roster preview, leader notes/logs, priority zone marking, preset defensive teams.

Import from benchmark:
- three-way map competition;
- prep/battle alternation;
- territory value;
- defense placement;
- controlled hero lock.

Do not import:
- 2v1 collusion without mitigation;
- excessive timezone pressure;
- winner-takes-all rewards;
- small guild suppression without consolation milestones.

## 13. Fame del Behemoth
Type: infinite guild boss.

Divine mechanics:
- Boss has progressive HP bars.
- Each HP bar is harder than the previous one.
- Rewards from bars destroyed and damage dealt.
- Daily.
- First battle manual required.
- Optional repeat/autoplay and extra entry rules must be carefully capped.

Benchmark inspiration:
- Raid Clan Boss chest tiers.
- Nikke Union Raid: test battle and attempt structure.
- Mythic Heroes guild daemons.
- Summoners War World Boss, only if roster-breadth is later desired.

Do not import:
- VIP auto-repeat as competitive advantage;
- gem extra entries deciding ranking;
- unreadable infinite scaling.

## 14. Furie del Pantheon
Type: guild elemental raid.

Divine mechanics:
- Opened by Guild Master or authorized guild roles.
- Costs guild activity points.
- Element-themed raid.
- Initial waves + final boss.
- Rewards: participation, damage, kill, utility rewards.
- Can be opened often when guild has enough activity points.
- Autoplay similar to Fame del Behemoth.

Benchmark inspiration:
- Nikke Union Raid.
- Mythic Heroes Wardragons / guild daemons.
- Omniheroes guild systems and synergy requirements, simplified.
- Summoners War Rift Raid contribution.

Do not import:
- guild leaders opening at bad times with no safeguards;
- element raids excluding most members;
- punitive activity-point economy.

## 15. Titanomachia
Confirmed benchmark: Bleach Online Protect Seireitei.

Type: major guild fortress siege.

Divine mechanics:
- One guild defends a fortress.
- Other guilds attack.
- Attack order: guardian → outer gate → inner gate.
- HP persists.
- Respawn timer: 60 seconds.
- Rewards: guild victory and individual leaderboard.
- Autoplay useful.
- Schedule: daily 15:30–16:30.

Import from benchmark:
- attacker/defender split;
- gates/walls;
- NPC/guardian layer;
- defending guild ownership/status;
- next defender/occupation logic if later approved.

Do not import:
- occupation bonuses too powerful;
- paid revive/skip;
- defender snowball;
- daily fortress fatigue without reward balancing.

## 16. Assalto del Ragnarök
Confirmed benchmark: Bleach Online Ryoka Attack.

Type: live wave attack event.

Divine mechanics:
- Waves of enemies cross the battlefield.
- Rewards for kills and non-lethal damage.
- Final rewards from wave reached and leaderboard.
- Autoplay useful.
- Schedule: 11:00–12:00 and 19:00–20:00.
- One rewarded participation per day across both windows.

Import from benchmark:
- wave pacing;
- damage ranking;
- participation rewards;
- threshold buffs if useful;
- revive timer.

Do not import:
- high final-blow reward;
- VIP auto/skip advantage;
- paid speedup/revive.

## Additional co-op mode: Scrigni dell’Elisio
Type: multiplayer/co-op jewel trial.

Divine mechanics:
- Always available.
- First 3 daily runs are rewarded.
- Later runs allow helping friends/guild without drops.
- Rewards based mainly on clear/completion.

Benchmark inspiration:
- Summoners War Rift Raid.
- SAO Memory Defrag party boss.
- Bleach co-op/Epic Raid.

Do not import:
- first-time friend reward exploit via alts;
- unlimited farming beyond daily cap.
